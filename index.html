Privacy policy

Plinth is a study app for Estate Management students. It is run by Joel Mathew (PHILEMON), the Plinth owner ("we", "us"). This page explains what we collect, why, who else handles it, and how you can control it.

In short

  • We collect only what the app needs: your name, matric number, email and what you do in the app.
  • We do not sell your data and Plinth shows no ads.
  • Your password is never visible to us. It is stored in a protected form by our sign-in provider.
  • Other students can see your name on anything you post in Community.
  • You can delete your account yourself, inside the app, at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details: full name, matric number, email address and password. Your matric number is checked against a list of verified students.
  • Study activity: drill and quiz attempts, timetable entries you add, and academic planning data you enter, such as CGPA results and goals. Depending on the feature, this is kept in your account or only on your phone.
  • Community content: discussions, replies, study group messages and reports you submit.
  • Device details for notifications: a push notification token that lets us send announcements to your phone.
  • Security records: sign-in attempt limits, which use scrambled identifiers, and a log of administrative actions.

Some things stay only on your phone and are not uploaded: SIWES log entries, your project outline, downloaded course files, and your theme and unlock settings.

Why we use it

  • To create your account, confirm you are a student of the department, and keep you signed in.
  • To show course materials, past questions, drills, your timetable and announcements.
  • To run Community and keep it safe, including reviewing reports.
  • To send verification codes and notifications you need.
  • To protect the app from misuse.

Who else handles your data

We use a few services to run Plinth. They process data only to provide their service to us.

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in system and file storage.
  • Resend sends verification and password reset emails.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications, where enabled.

We do not sell personal data. We may share information if the law requires it.

Who can see what

  • The Plinth owner can see account details and moderate Community content.
  • Other signed-in students can see your name with the discussions, replies and group messages you post.
  • Your study activity, timetable and settings are private to you and the owner.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile, sign-in, posts, replies, messages and study records are removed. Records of administrative and moderation actions may remain, with your name and account link cleared. Copies in system backups can persist for a short period before they are overwritten.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, object to how it is used, and receive a copy of it. You can delete your account in the app, or write to us using the contact details below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and access is limited by account and role. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your phone locked.

Age

Plinth is for university students. It is not meant for anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.

Terms of use

By creating an account or using Plinth, you agree to these terms.

Who can use Plinth

Plinth is for students on the verified list. You must give accurate details, use only your own account, and keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

Study materials

Course materials and past questions are provided for your own study. They belong to their original authors and lecturers. Do not copy, sell or share them outside Plinth.

Estate Management tools

The calculators, valuation tables, formulas and notes are for learning. Results are estimates, not a formal valuation, a mortgage offer, or financial or legal advice. Check your assumptions and follow your lecturers and professional rules before relying on any figure.

Community rules

You keep ownership of what you post. You allow Plinth to display it to other students within the app.

Changes and governing law

Delete your account

You can delete your Plinth account and data yourself. This cannot be undone.

In the app

  1. Sign in and open the menu in the top left.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and enter your password.
  4. Tap Delete my account.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, sign-in, matric number link and email on your account.
  • Your quiz attempts, timetable entries, notification token and reports you filed.
  • Your discussions, replies and study group messages. Study groups you created stay for other members, without a named creator.
  • Anything stored on your phone for Plinth, such as SIWES entries and downloaded files.

Records of administrative actions may remain with your name cleared. After deletion you can register again later if your matric number is still on the verified list.

If you cannot open the app

Email us from your registered address with your full name and matric number, and ask us to delete your account. We will confirm your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.
